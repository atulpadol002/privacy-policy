Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how [APP NAME] ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our caller and contacts application.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. The App provides calling, contacts, call history, caller identification, and related phone functionality.

1. Information and Permissions We Access

Contacts

READ_CONTACTS

This permission is used to display contacts in the App, search contacts, identify callers using your contacts, and allow you to select contacts when making calls.

WRITE_CONTACTS

This permission allows you to add, edit, or delete contacts through the App.

Call History

READ_CALL_LOG

This permission is used to display recent incoming, outgoing, and missed calls.

WRITE_CALL_LOG

Where supported, this permission allows you to delete call-log entries using features provided by the App.

Phone State and Phone Information

READ_PHONE_STATE

This permission may be used to detect phone and call states so the App can provide caller and call-related functionality.

READ_PHONE_NUMBERS

This permission may be used for phone-number-related functionality, including supported multi-SIM functionality and caller identification.

Calling

CALL_PHONE

This permission allows the App to initiate a phone call when you select a contact or phone number and request a call.

ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS

Where supported by the device and Android version, this permission allows the App's incoming-call interface to provide call-answer functionality.

MANAGE_OWN_CALLS

This permission may be used to integrate supported calling functionality with the Android Telecom framework.

SMS

SEND_SMS

This permission is used for the optional Quick SMS Reply feature when you choose to send an SMS while rejecting an incoming call.

The App does not intentionally send SMS messages without an action initiated by you.

Notifications

POST_NOTIFICATIONS

This permission is used to display notifications related to incoming calls, active calls, missed calls, and other relevant call-related events.

Full-Screen Incoming Call Interface

USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT

Where permitted by Android, this capability may be used to display the incoming-call interface prominently, including when the device is locked.

Foreground Call Service

FOREGROUND_SERVICE_PHONE_CALL

This capability may be used during an active call so that required call-related functionality can continue while the App is running in the background.

Audio Settings

MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS

This permission may be used to provide call audio controls such as speakerphone, earpiece, mute, and audio routing functionality.

Wake Lock

WAKE_LOCK

The App may use a wake lock during relevant call functionality to help keep the device awake when required.

Audio Files

READ_MEDIA_AUDIO

Where supported, this permission may be used to allow you to select audio files for supported custom ringtone functionality.
